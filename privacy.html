Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

LoanCheckBLR is a free loan eligibility check run from Bengaluru by Shivam Kendre. We are not a bank or NBFC, and we do not give loans. This page explains what we collect and why, in plain words.

What we collect

  • Your name and mobile number.
  • Your answers in the check: loan type, age range, income range, existing EMIs, CIBIL range, missed EMIs, settlement, property details and the loan amount you need.
  • The language you chose and the date and time of your check.

We do not ask for PAN, Aadhaar, bank account details, passwords or OTPs. We do not pull your credit report, so this check does not affect your CIBIL score.

Why we collect it

  • To show your loan result.
  • So a LoanCheckBLR advisor can call you once to help, unless you ticked "Don't call me".
  • If you tell us you want to apply, to share your details with a bank, NBFC or registered loan agent (DSA) so they can process your loan.

Who we share it with

Only with a bank, NBFC or loan agent, and only after you tell us you want to apply. We never sell your number or details, and we never share them for advertising.

How long we keep it

Up to 12 months after your check, then we delete it. If you apply for a loan through us, we keep the details the lender or agent needs for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

  • Ask us what data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct it or delete it.
  • Withdraw your permission for calls at any time.

Email YOUR-EMAIL@example.com or WhatsApp YOUR-NUMBER with your name and mobile number. We reply within 7 days. If you are not happy with our reply, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

How we protect it

Your details are stored in a private Google Sheet that only LoanCheckBLR can open. The website itself does not keep your details.
