        How the estimate works

        • EMI limit (FOIR): all EMIs together may use 40% of income up to ₹25,000 a month, 50% up to ₹50,000, 55% up to ₹1 lakh and 60% above that. Lenders publish 40–65% (Axis about 40%, ICICI 45–50%, SBI up to 60%).
        • Interest rate: picked by CIBIL band from lenders' published starting rates: personal 11–24%, business 17–26%, home 8–10.5%, LAP 9.5–14%.
        • Tenure: personal 5 years, business 3 years, home up to 30 years, LAP up to 15 years, cut short so the loan ends by age 65 (salaried) or 70 (self-employed).
        • Home loan cap: RBI loan-to-value limits: 90% up to ₹30 lakh, 80% up to ₹75 lakh, 75% above.
        • LAP cap: 60% of a residential and 50% of a commercial property's value (lenders go up to 75%).
        • Business loan cap: 20% of yearly turnover, a rule of thumb for unsecured loans.
        • Suggested login: with CIBIL 700+ and no bounces, banks come first (cheapest rate), then housing finance companies and NBFCs. With a weaker profile, NBFCs come first, then fintech apps. A bank where the salary is credited gets top priority. Log in with one lender at a time: each application is a hard enquiry on the customer's CIBIL report.
        • Lead score (0–100): CIBIL 35, EMI room 25, no bounces 15, job/business stability 15, employer or ITR record 10. Settled or written-off loans cap the score at 35. Hot is 75+, warm 55–74, cold below 55 or no lender match.
